# Relics

Relics are powerful items that can be bound to gear with a relic socket.

Relics do not appear in chest loot. They are boss drops only.

## Using Relics

1. Add a relic socket with a Relic Socket Inscription.
2. Put the socketed gear and relic into the Artisan's Workbench.
3. Bind the relic to the gear.

Relics usually add corruption and increase durability use.

## Boss Sources

Dragon Heart: dropped by the Ender Dragon.

Elder Guardian's Eye: dropped by Elder Guardians.

Wither Charge: dropped by the Wither.

Warden's Soul: dropped by Wardens.

## Dragon Heart

Empowers fire-based effects and rewards aggressive burning builds.

Full set power: press the relic keybind to breathe dragon fire.

## Elder Guardian's Eye

Improves underwater combat and mining, with stronger benefits while fighting in water.

Full set power: press the relic keybind to fire a guardian beam.

## Wither Charge

Extends wither effects and increases damage against withered enemies.

Full set power: press the relic keybind to launch a wither bullet.

## Warden's Soul

Improves damage against powerful enemies and rewards fighting high-health targets.

Full set power: press the relic keybind to release a sonic boom.

## Relic Sets

Relic set bonuses can trigger when enough equipped items use the same relic. The active relic power has a cooldown.
