# Inscriptions

Inscriptions are utility items used in the Artisan's Workbench.

## Expansion Inscription

Adds an enhancement slot. This usually costs durability and corruption.

## Restoration Inscription

Reduces corruption, but can lower maximum durability.

## Nullification Inscription

Removes enhancements. It may damage capacity and increase corruption.

## Upgrade Inscription

Improves an existing stat enhancement. It increases corruption and can mark the item as overforged.

## Reroll Inscription

Rerolls a stat enhancement. Better results may make the item unstable.

## Extraction Inscription

Extracts an enhancement from gear. This can seal the item and increase corruption.

## Purification Inscription

Removes a negative attribute. It increases corruption and can reduce durability.

## Stabilization Inscription

Stabilizes an item, usually at the cost of brittleness and corruption.

## Tempering Inscription

Reinforces an item to reduce durability loss, while adding corruption.

## Relic Socket Inscription

Adds a relic socket so one relic can be bound later.

## Wild and Cursed Inscriptions

Wild inscriptions mutate enhancements into something else.

Cursed inscriptions attempt to force power beyond normal limits. Failure can curse and damage the item.
